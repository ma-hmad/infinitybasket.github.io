How it started

Built around one simple idea

Infinity Basket started with a simple idea: a neighbourhood shouldn't have to choose between fresh and affordable. We work directly with local farms and trusted suppliers so every basket that leaves our store is stocked with quality — at prices that make sense for everyday life.

From produce restocked each morning to pantry staples that never run low, we built Infinity Basket to be the store you can rely on for the shop you make most: the everyday one.
